Dimensions are rounded to the nearest whole pixel, so a row's width × height can differ from the exact megapixel count by a fraction of a percent. Real sensors also use their own exact dimensions — a "12 MP" iPhone photo is actually 4032 × 3024 (12.19 MP), close to but not exactly the 4000 × 3000 in the 4:3 column.

How the conversion works

For a ratio of W:H, the width is √(megapixels × 1,000,000 × W ÷ H) and the height is the width × H ÷ W. Example, 24 MP at 3:2: √(24,000,000 × 3 ÷ 2) = √36,000,000 = 6000 px wide, and 6000 × 2 ÷ 3 = 4000 px high — the classic 6000 × 4000 file.

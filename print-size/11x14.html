Pixels Needed for an 11×14 Print

For a sharp 11×14 inch print at 300 PPI you need 3300 × 4200 pixels (13.86 megapixels). At 200 PPI — a practical target for a framed wall print — you need 2200 × 2800 pixels (6.16 MP).

Pixels needed for 11×14, by quality level

ResolutionPixels neededMegapixelsQuality
300 PPI3300 × 4200 px13.9 MPExcellent — close viewing
200 PPI2200 × 2800 px6.2 MPGood — normal distance
150 PPI1650 × 2100 px3.5 MPAcceptable — wall viewing

The maths is print inches × PPI for each side: 11 in × 300 = 3300 px and 14 in × 300 = 4200 px, and 3300 × 4200 = 13,860,000 pixels = 13.86 megapixels (rounded to 13.9 MP above). The 200 and 150 PPI rows are 6.16 MP and 3.465 MP before rounding.

Crop and aspect ratio: 11×14 is 11:14

11×14 has an 11:14 aspect ratio — squarer than a standard photo, between 4:5 (8×10, 16×20) and 5:7. Expect a crop from most originals:

  • A typical DSLR or mirrorless photo is 3:2 — a 6000 × 4000 px file crops to 5091 × 4000 px to fill 11×14, losing 909 px (15.2%) from the long side.
  • A typical phone photo is 4:3 — closer in shape, so the crop is small: a 4032 × 3024 px file crops to 3849 × 3024 px, losing 183 px (4.5%) from the long side.
